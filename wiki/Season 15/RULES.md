# Vigilante Racing League — Season 15 rules

Status: league-owner supplied on July 27, 2026.

- 17-week season.
- 12 regular-season races followed by a five-race Chase.
- As in Season 14, there is no “win and you're in” qualification rule (NASCAR 2026 format).
- Two drop weeks apply during races 1–12.
- Hard Charger earns three bonus points.
- A zero-incident-points race earns two bonus points.
- A driver who receives a penalty during the league owner's post-race review does not receive the zero-incident bonus.

## Evidence boundary

These are owner-supplied competition rules, not facts inferred from broadcasts. This file does not establish a Season 15 champion, current standings, penalty decisions, race-by-race incident counts, or which two weeks any driver will drop. Those outcomes remain separate and require their own reviewed league record or official-source receipt.
