# SHOKKER LORE

**The creator memory world: every episode remembered, every moment findable,
every fan deeper in.**

SHOKKER LORE turns a large social-video or podcast archive into three connected
products:

1. a searchable, source-linked memory system;
2. a channel-native fan experience; and
3. a creator studio for resurfacing stories and planning clips.

This folder productizes the lessons proven by the VRL Living Wiki and WWAM
After Midnight without turning future clients into reskins of either one.

## The pitch

> Your back catalog stops being a graveyard and starts being a second channel.

## The product rule

The same invisible memory spine may power every client. The visible product
must feel authored for exactly one creator.

If a finished surface could be moved to an unrelated channel by changing its
logo, color, and noun labels, it is not SHOKKER LORE yet.
