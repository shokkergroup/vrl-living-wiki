# HLRN Living Wiki

The public, source-linked living archive of the
[High Line Racing Network](https://www.youtube.com/@High_Line_Racing).

**Public site:** https://shokkergroup.github.io/vrl-living-wiki/hlrn/

## Archive snapshot

- 52 livestream identities
- 20 official race files: Season 1 (16) and Season 2 (4)
- 29 complete bonus broadcasts in **Highline Live**
- 3 fragments retained in the Source Ledger
- 20 HLRN-authored companion files mapped to official races
- 99 normalized driver dossiers
- 42 source-attributed driver-frame dossiers with honest fallbacks for the rest
- 357 full-broadcast race chapters, with 15-18 distinct cuts per official race
- 83 editor-reviewed Central / `The Show` receipts with 83 unique titles
- 424 machine-surfaced research candidates quarantined from public editorial
- 20/20 official races with an embedded primary player, dense chapter board,
  timed transcript, editorial receipts, and Central edition
- 20 tape-supported winners and a channel-supported Season 1 champion receipt

## Core experience

The VRL engine's evidence architecture is preserved—stable source identities,
lazy timed transcripts, exact YouTube recovery links, unknown-safe result
boundaries, hash routes, and explainable rankings—but HLRN has its own visual
world and features: **Highline Central**, **The Show** companion shelf,
**Race Radar**, **Pack Finder**, **Highline Frequency**, **Last Lap Lottery**,
and the separate **Highline Live** bonus lane.

Every official race file now opens around the full broadcast itself. Its
15-18 chapter cards seek the embedded primary race at an exact start time,
carry a bounded end time, and deep-link back to that timestamp. Companion-show
and Central receipts remain visible as a separate story layer; they never stand
in for the full-race chapter board.

The expanded experience now adds a **Results Room**, **Winner’s Garage**,
**Visual Garage**, **Photo Desk**, **Driver Compare**, reviewed-only
**Battle Lines**, **Track Atlas**, **Signal Timeline**, **Finish Vault**,
authored **Story Paths**, **Race Night Mixer**, persistent **Replay Builder**,
**Highline Lore Studio**, **Highline Pulse**, **Evidence Ledger**,
**Open Records**, **Corrections Desk**, and **Owner Result Intake**. The current
audit measures 38 HLRN route families versus 36 in the VRL reference.

Full finishing orders, starts, points, and standings remain open until owner
records are supplied. Every published winner or podium is backed by a
position-specific call in a primary HLRN broadcast or HLRN-authored companion.
